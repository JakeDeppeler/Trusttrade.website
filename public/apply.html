Trust Trade tradie agreement

1. Who you are

You confirm you're an authorised representative of the business named in this application, and that the ABN, licence, insurance and other details you've given are true, current and your own.

2. Verification

Trust Trade verifies your ABN, trade licence and public liability insurance against the issuing bodies before you go live, and re-checks them from time to time. You'll keep these current and tell us if anything changes, lapses or is cancelled.

3. How you'll work

  • Do the work you're licensed and competent to do, to the standards of your trade and the law.
  • Honour the prices and quotes you give customers. All prices on your profile are shown plus GST.
  • Turn up when you say you will, communicate honestly, and treat customers and their homes with respect.
  • Handle your own tax, super and insurance — Trust Trade is a listing and introduction platform, not your employer.

4. Reviews & profile

Customer reviews are theirs, not yours — you won't write, buy, or pressure for fake reviews. You grant Trust Trade permission to display your business details and the photos you upload on your public profile.

5. Fees

Any subscription or lead fees will be set out clearly before you're charged. You can cancel in line with the plan you choose.

6. Removal

False licence, insurance or identity details, or conduct that puts customers at risk, means immediate removal from the platform. We may also suspend or remove listings that breach this agreement or the law.
