Trust Trade Pro

Tools for tradies, on your phone

Trust Trade Pro puts Trust Trade's trade tools in one app. Ducted Designer is for air-conditioning installers: scan a home with an iPhone Pro or upload the builder's plan, and it works out every room's heat load, sizes the unit and lays out the ducts. It then writes the customer report and the materials list.

Getting help

Email jake@trusttrade.au with your question. Screenshots and the job's report help.

Common questions

  • Which iPhones can scan? Room scanning needs LiDAR (iPhone Pro models). Every other iPhone on iOS 17 or later can upload a plan or try the demo house.
  • Do I need an account to read a plan? No. Plans are read on Trust Trade's server with Anthropic's Claude, and each phone can read up to 10 plans a day.
  • Where are my jobs saved? On your iPhone only. Share the PDF or CSV from a job's Reports step to send it anywhere.
  • How accurate are the loads? They're design estimates using the Ducted Designer method. Check them against AIRAH DA09 and the manufacturer's data before you commit.

Privacy

Jobs and customer details stay on your phone. Plan images go to Anthropic's Claude only when you choose to read a plan. Read the Trust Trade Pro Privacy Policy.
